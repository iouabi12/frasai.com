Delete Your FrasAI Account and Data

FrasAI – language learning app by Ismail Ouabi

Delete your account from inside the app

  1. Open FrasAI on your device.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This removes your account and all learning data stored on your device, and deletes the copy held on our servers in the same step. Nothing further is needed.

If your device is offline at that moment, the app tells you so and the server copy stays until you repeat the step or write to us.

Ask us to delete it instead

If you no longer have the app installed, or the deletion inside the app did not go through, send us a request from the email address you used:

support@frasai.com

Please include the subject line “Account deletion request”. We will confirm the deletion by email within 30 days of receiving your request.

What gets deleted, what is kept

DataWhat happens
Email address Deleted
Display name Deleted
Learning progress (levels, solved sentences, word status) Deleted
Feedback you submitted Kept in anonymised form – it contains no email address or name and cannot be traced back to you
Subscription and payment records Handled by Google Play, not by us. Retained by Google as required by tax and accounting law. Manage your subscription in the Google Play Store.
Deleting your data does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

Data we never store

Your answers to exercises are sent to OpenAI for evaluation and are returned as corrections. They are not linked to your email address or to any identifier, and we do not keep them after the correction is shown.
